Dice Function (Analog Search)

Because DOS cannot run modern JavaScript search engines, this archive uses an analog search method: rolling two standard 6-sided dice (2d6).

Step 1: Find your starting letter.
Roll the dice. Your first die is the ROW, and your second die is the COLUMN. Find the square where they meet on the grid below and click it.

Row 4[ S ][ T ][ U ][ V ][ W ][ X ]
Row 5[ Y ][ Z ][ 0 ][ 1 ][ 2 ][ 3 ]
Row 6[ 4 ][ 5 ][ 6 ][ 7 ][ 8 ][ 9 ]

Step 2: Pick your sub-category.
Once you click a letter, you will see a list of two-letter combinations (like [ Ca ] or [ Ce ]). Because the number of combinations varies by letter, the dice cannot help you here. Simply browse the grid and click the combination that catches your eye.

Step 3: What if I land on a Number?
If you land on a number, it will take you to the Number Index. Because standard 6-sided dice cannot roll a 0, 7, 8, or 9, the dice function ends here. Simply browse the Number Index manually to find your two-digit combination (e.g., [ 15 ] or [ 42 ]).
